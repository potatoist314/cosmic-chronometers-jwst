# Quiescent-galaxy stellar populations (LEGA-C focus)

Source: arXiv PDF abstracts; authoritative index: `../README.md`.

## Beverage et al. (2021) - Mass-size plane

- 65 massive quiescent LEGA-C galaxies, 0.59<=z<=0.75; alf full-spectrum modelling.
- [Mg/H] and [Fe/H] correlate with M*/Re above 10^10.5 Msun; compact galaxies are metal-richer.
- [Mg/Fe] varies with neither mass nor M*/Re; marginal positive age-mass relation.
- Star-formation timescale independent of mass/size; mass-[Fe/H] and mass-[Mg/H] relations ~0.2 dex below z~0.

## Beverage et al. (2023) - From Carbon to Cobalt

- 135 quiescent LEGA-C galaxies, z~0.7; four 150-250 km/s dispersion bins; hierarchical Bayesian full-spectrum fitting.
- C, N, Mg, Si, Ca, Ti, V, Cr, Fe, Co, Ni: 0.05 dex precision.
- Metallicity, age and most [X/H] rise with sigma; [X/Fe] trends mild or absent.
- Patterns resemble z~0; lowest-sigma bin forms earlier, with slightly enhanced N, Mg, Ti, Ni.

## Bevacqua et al. (2023) - [alpha/Fe] non-evolution

- 183 quiescent LEGA-C galaxies, z=0.60-0.75, 10.4<=log M*<=11.6; Mgb/Fe4383 compared with SSP models.
- 91% supersolar; mean [alpha/Fe] = +0.24 +/- 0.01.
- [alpha/Fe] shows no significant correlation with metallicity, mass, velocity dispersion or formation time.
- Mean [alpha/Fe] matches z=0.38, z=0.07 stacks and local early types; unchanged over ~6.5 Gyr.

## Bevacqua et al. (2024) - MZR new perspective

- 637 quiescent LEGA-C galaxies, 0.6<=z<=1, 10.4<=log M*<11.7; full-spectrum mass-weighted metallicities.
- Low-mass galaxies span metal-rich to metal-poor; high-mass galaxies exclude metal-poor populations.
- Metallicity has a mass-dependent lower limit, the MEME zone; MgFe confirms this bound.
- Galaxies formed at z>=3 reproduce bound; SFH after first Gyr barely changes metallicity.

## Gallazzi et al. (2026a) - Scaling relations I

- 552 LEGA-C galaxies, 0.6<z<0.77, mass >1e10 Msun; absorption indices plus rizYJ photometry.
- Local downsizing trends already established 6 Gyr ago.
- Light-weighted age bimodality transitions near ~1e11 Msun; metallicity-mass changes steep-to-flat across 10^10.8 Msun.
- Velocity-dispersion trends match; sharper young-to-old transition at log sigma = 2.3.

## Gallazzi et al. (2026b) - Scaling relations II

- Same 552 galaxies split quiescent/star-forming; compared with aperture-corrected SDSS DR7.
- Different quiescent and star-forming age distributions drive age bimodality.
- Quiescent metallicity barely evolves since z=0.7; lower-mass star-forming galaxies are metal-poorer than local counterparts.
- Median ages change only ~2 Gyr from z=0.7 to z=0.1, below passive ageing.

## Cheng et al. (2025) - UVJ vs absorption lines

- ~700 quiescent LEGA-C galaxies, 0.6<=z<=1.0, 10.2<log M*<11.8; alf fits continuum-normalised spectra.
- Age increases with redder U-V, as models predict.
- [Fe/H] increases perpendicular to age: redder U-V and bluer V-J.
- Dynamical/model M/L differences suggest non-solar abundance patterns or evolved-star modelling gaps.

## Conroy et al. (2014) - z~0 anchor

- SDSS early-type stacks, sigma 90-300 km/s; 4000-8800 A full-spectrum fitting with 16 elements.
- Light-weighted ages rise from 6-12 Gyr with sigma.
- [Fe/H] varies <0.1 dex; [Mg/Fe] and [O/Fe] rise from ~0.0 to ~0.25 with sigma.
- Ca/V/Cr/Mn/Ni track Fe; Co tracks O; [C/Fe] and [N/Fe] exceed 0.2 at high sigma.

## Borghi et al. (2022a) - Passive LEGA-C populations

- 140 passive LEGA-C galaxies at z~0.7; Lick indices via PyLick; paper in `../chronometer/`.
- Median [Z/H] = 0.08 +/- 0.18.
- Median [alpha/Fe] = 0.13 +/- 0.11.
- Ages follow cosmic ageing; downsizing.
