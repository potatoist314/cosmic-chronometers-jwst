Modal's complete outer notebook wall time was 3 h 2 min 28 s. At the recorded Vast price of $0.670/hour, the 4,138.2-second sampler cost about $0.77.

Why one iteration has a different cost

One outer iteration removes several live points. Each replacement uses several inner slice-sampling steps. Their product gives the recorded likelihood-call count per outer iteration.

Iteration time alone therefore exaggerates the difference. Calls per second is a better normalization, but the likelihoods still use different data and grid schemas.

What these numbers can answer

  • Both providers ran Ceridwen with CUDA and JAX float64.
  • The retained full runs sustained about 100–120 likelihood calls/s.
  • The Vast allocation completed the joint configuration successfully.
  • The Modal checkpoint preserved useful state after an interrupted run.

Project synthesis: These runs cannot identify a provider winner or isolate FP32 throughput, FP64 throughput, VRAM capacity, or memory bandwidth. That requires the same likelihood, grid, sampler settings, and profiler on each GPU.
