<summary>Caveats</summary>

- kcbump has \(\delta_{\mathrm{dust}}\) median \(-0.987\) at the \(-1\) prior bound and \(E_{\mathrm{bump}}\) median \(5.86\) at the \(6\) prior bound.
- m3 has \(\delta_{\mathrm{dust}}=-2.36\), inside its \(\mathrm{Uniform}(-3,0.4)\) prior; its \(E_{\mathrm{bump}}\) is not a free parameter.
- The fits differ in law and bump, \(\delta_{\mathrm{dust}}\) prior, birth-cloud dust, SFH bins, Ceridwen commit and seed.
- Stored spectral \(\chi^2\) uses each fit’s own \(f_{\mathrm{calib}}\); the table reports both at the kcbump floor.
