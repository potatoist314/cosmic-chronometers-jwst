#### Format

Short updates last 3–4 minutes. This is a good time to introduce people: they know XXX and this might be helpful.

#### Questions after the updates

- What is the turnover in star formation? The star-forming main sequence is linear, then plateaus.
- What is the SFMS? It is the star-forming main sequence. The main sequence scales as \((1+z)\): what does this mean?
- What is an IFU? What is a spaxel?
- What is an APT file? What are Illustris and TNG?
- What are CIRCI [?] and Kron photometry? H\(\alpha\) probes \(\sim 10^{1}\) Myr [as written] of star-formation history; the infrared probes \(10^{7}\) Myr [?]. Do different wavelengths probe different star-formation histories?

#### Questions I need help with, outside SED fitting

- Are there more ground-truth sources for the LEGA-C catalogue? \(\alpha/\mathrm{Fe}\) looks too low. Is anyone working with spectroscopically calibrated LEGA-C data?
- Can I get more help with COSMOS photometry calibration: the Laigle offset? Does anyone have experience with spectrophotometry in general?
- Should I incorporate metallicity-history evolution? Should I use more free priors for SED fitting?
- Should I make KL-divergence plots?
- I need more help with sensible quiescent priors. Can I get more advice on dust, e.g. emission in quiescent galaxies? Should I consider AGN?
- What other parameters can I fit for \(\alpha\)-enhanced passive quiescent galaxies that are poorly constrained?
- Is there fitting I can do outside LEGA-C, like JWST? What about JWST masking?

#### Points raised

- Talk to Jonah about quiescent galaxies. Add Jonah and post the model assumptions. Is there an automated way to print the model?
- There is a huge amount of ex-situ mass once star formation shuts down, around \(\log_{10}(M_\star/M_\odot) \approx 10.8\) (stellar or luminosity mass, not total?). The extra mass all comes from mergers.
- Use stacking analysis or SDSS for better ground truth.
- \(\alpha\)-enhancement is not surprising.
- Summarise statistics: \(\alpha/\mathrm{Fe}\) against stellar mass, \(t_{50}\) against stellar mass. A relative trend can be constrained even if you only have the distribution.
- ★ List the parameters in Slack.
- Is there a spaxel 3D spectral-cube visualiser for a galaxy?
- IFU is unbiased compared with slit spectroscopy.
- What about bulge–disk separation and decomposition? Super hard.
