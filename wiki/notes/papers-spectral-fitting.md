<details>
<summary>Park et al. (2025) - Alpha-MC</summary>

- Solar-scaled models can bias ages of alpha-enhanced high-redshift galaxies.
- Isochrones and spectra push colors and indices in opposite directions.
- Alpha-enhancement shifts broadband colors by 0.1-0.4 magnitudes.
- Balmer age indicators depend on alpha abundance and iron abundance.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Tacchella et al. (2022) - Fast, Slow, Early, Late</summary>

- Massive quiescent galaxies follow no single quenching pathway.
- One quarter of the sample quenches within 500 million years.
- Early formers assemble faster; late formers assemble slower.
- Continuous quenching diversity points to internal and external mechanisms.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Wan et al. (2025) - SFH variability</summary>

- Main-sequence scatter survives two-gigayear averaging: not mere flickering.
- Galaxies stay above or below the main sequence for roughly one gigayear.
- Short fluctuations add 0.1-0.2 dex atop long-term tracks.
- Scatter may mix halo history with rapid gas self-regulation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why this matters here</summary>

- Alpha abundance errors can bias inferred mass-weighted ages.
- Rigid SFHs risk erasing real quenching diversity and gigayear memory.

</details>
