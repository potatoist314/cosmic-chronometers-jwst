<details><summary>Definitions against Ceridwen</summary>

- Metallicity: total \([\mathrm{Z}/\mathrm{H}]\) or \([\mathrm{M}/\mathrm{H}]\) against Ceridwen \([\mathrm{Fe}/\mathrm{H}]\). Thomas+2003: \([\mathrm{Z}/\mathrm{H}]=[\mathrm{Fe}/\mathrm{H}]+0.94[\alpha/\mathrm{Fe}]\); difference 0.24 dex at \([\alpha/\mathrm{Fe}]=+0.25\).
- Solar scale: Ceridwen \(Z_\odot=0.0185\); Gallazzi+2026 0.01524; others model-dependent.
- Age: SSP-equivalent and light-weighted younger than mass-weighted ages (McDermid+2015). Local ages minus lookback time to \(z=0.7\), about 6.4 Gyr under Planck 2018: expected age only under passive evolution; progenitor bias, mergers, late star formation.
- Mass: Ceridwen formed mass; literature surviving stars plus remnants. Salpeter about 0.25 dex above Chabrier (Auger+2010). Dynamical mass: aperture total, including dark matter.
- Aperture: SDSS fibre \(0.4\text{–}0.8R_e\) at \(z\sim0.05\); CvD12 and MASSIVE \(R_e/8\); ATLAS3D, SAMI, MaNGA, LEGA-C \(1R_e\). Choi+2014: \(\leq0.06\) dex \([\mathrm{Fe}/\mathrm{H}]\) bias from -0.23 dex per decade gradient.
- Data release: DR2 against DR3, about 0.2 dex in \([\mathrm{Fe}/\mathrm{H}]\), \([\mathrm{Mg}/\mathrm{H}]\) (Beverage+2023).
- Selection: UVJ (LEGA-C); morphology (Thomas+2010, ATLAS3D); emission-line and Fundamental-Plane cuts (Conroy+2014); sSFR (Choi+2014).
